🧭 What are Routing Aliases?

A Routing Alias (like auto, fast, vision, strong) is a named routing profile defined in ~/.llmhub/providers.yaml. When your app asks for "model": "auto", the gateway rotates calls across the models in its preference list, spreading traffic across the top candidates according to the spread setting (least-recently-used). You can edit any alias profile below to reorder preferences, add or remove models, and tune candidate spread.
