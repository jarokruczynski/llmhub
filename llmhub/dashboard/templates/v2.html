Routing & Matrix

Aliases pick a free model per request and fall back down their preference list when a vendor says no.

A routing alias (auto, fast, vision, strong) is a profile from ~/.llmhub/providers.yaml. A call with "model": "auto" rotates across the alias's preference list, spreading traffic over the top spread candidates, least recently used first. Edit a profile below to reorder, add or remove models.
